Research focus

From circuits
to cognition.

My work brings together a foundation in control systems and power engineering with modern robotics and machine learning. I design reproducible pipelines that connect sensing, estimation, planning, and actuation.

Currently completing an MSc in Robotics & Artificial Intelligence at ITMO University with a Red Diploma, I am interested in robust autonomy that can move from simulation into real-world deployment.

Three connected fields of practice: understanding the scene, choosing the path, and controlling the system.

1

Robot perception

LiDAR-camera fusion, object detection, 3D reconstruction, and scene understanding form the sensing layer of my work. I combine calibrated projection, segmentation, clustering, and geometric reasoning to turn raw observations into usable spatial structure. The aim is a perception pipeline that is training-free where possible, measurable in practice, and ready for autonomous decisions.

2

Autonomous navigation

I study multi-layer planning and Bayesian estimation for constrained, dynamic environments. Global routes, local avoidance, and state estimation are treated as connected parts of one navigation system rather than isolated algorithms. This systems view supports robust movement from a simulated world toward real-world deployment.

A*DWAUKF
↗
3

Intelligent control

Model-based control and systems thinking connect my robotics research to my electrical-engineering foundation. I work with MPC, PID, power systems, and inverter-based modelling to reason about stability, actuation, and performance. The goal is intelligent control that respects the physical system it is asked to move.
